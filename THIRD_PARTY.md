## Microsoft ML for Beginners / AI for Beginners (MIT)

see `tools/build_ms_lessons.mjs`) are converted from the Russian translations of:

- <https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners> — `translations/ru`
- <https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners> — `translations/ru`
